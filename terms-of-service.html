2. Description of Service

Udana is an iOS app for people who take yoga and fitness classes, their teachers, and home practitioners. The Service provides:

  • Class memory — on-device audio recognition during live classes, including song identification and the theme and cues of the teacher's words.
  • Class Experience — post-class review with a class timeline, playlist, movement sequence, and workout data.
  • Teachers & music — teacher pages, a library of the songs from your classes, and search.
  • Health & Performance — HealthKit integration, heart-rate zone analysis, and workout trends.

Additional features include an Apple Watch companion, Spotify and Apple Music playlist export, Strava activity export, Siri shortcuts, Dynamic Island Live Activity, share cards for social media (including Instagram Stories), Apple Health workout writing, venue auto-tagging, nearby peer discovery, and the optional Udana Pro subscription.
