Questions

What kinds of classes does it work with?

Any class with music and a teacher: vinyasa, hot yoga, yin, Pilates, barre, spin and HIIT. Udāna is made yoga-first, so class types, poses and themes speak that language.

Is the sound of my class saved?

No. During class, Udāna uses the microphone to recognize each song and gather your teacher's words. Sound is understood on your iPhone in the moment, then let go. It's never saved or uploaded.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Everything works on iPhone alone. With Apple Watch you also get your heart rate through the class and can start and end class from your wrist.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no server holding your classes. Your library lives on your iPhone.

Can I take my playlists to other apps?

Yes. Open any song in Apple Music or Spotify, save a class playlist to either, or send the workout to Strava. Udāna sends only what you choose, straight from your iPhone.

How do I cancel Udāna Pro?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Udāna Pro. Cancel a free trial at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged.
