Udana

Tap once. Relive every moment.

Udana is an intelligent workout class companion that listens to the boutique fitness class you just took and hands back the full experience. You get the playlist with timestamps, the cues your instructor shouted over the bridge, and the shape of the session from warmup to cooldown — all reconstructed on-device from audio that's never stored.

Class Capture Engine

ShazamKit, Speech, and SoundAnalysis run in parallel to recognize songs, transcribe cues, and classify the session as it happens.

Class Experience

Review the class after it ends — a timeline, the full playlist with timestamps, the movement sequence, and your workout data.

Instructor Cues, Isolated

Apple Music lyrics are subtracted from live speech so you hear your instructor, not the song. FoundationModels polishes the transcript on-device.

Health & Performance

HealthKit integration surfaces heart-rate zones and workout trends alongside the class itself, so effort has context.

Community & Identity

Instructor profiles, shared class experiences, and a cross-studio social layer that doesn't exist anywhere else.

Private by Design

Audio is ephemeral and never permanently stored. Recognition runs on-device across Apple frameworks only — no third-party SDKs.
