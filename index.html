The memory of every class you take.

When class begins, tap Start and put your phone away. Afterwards you'll have what you'd otherwise forget: every song in order, the theme your teacher wove through class, the lines worth keeping, and how your body moved from the first breath to final rest. Class by class, it becomes the story of your practice.

The soundtrack of your practice

Every song, in the order it played, ready to save to Apple Music or Spotify. Over time, every song that carried you through class, gathered in one place.

The words that stayed with you

The theme your teacher wove through class and the lines worth keeping, with song lyrics left out so you hold onto what your teacher said.

The teachers you return to

A page for each teacher: the songs they love, the themes and phrases they come back to, and every class you've shared.

Every heartbeat

Heart rate and recovery from Apple Watch alongside the class, and a monthly look at who you practised with and the music that moved you.

Share the class

Turn any class into a card, with a photo from the studio if you like, and share it to your Instagram Story or anywhere else.

Private by design

During class, sound is understood on your iPhone in the moment, then let go. It's never saved or uploaded. Your library lives on your iPhone, with no account and no ads.
