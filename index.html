Udana

The memory of every class you take.

Set your iPhone by your mat and tap Start. Udana listens on-device and keeps the class: every song in order, the cues and words your teacher came back to, and your heart rate from Apple Watch. Class after class, it becomes a library of your practice — your teachers, the music you've heard, and any moment you want to find again. Audio is processed on your iPhone and never stored.

Every song, in order

The class playlist with timestamps, ready to save to Apple Music or Spotify — and a list of every song you've heard in class, most-heard first.

Your teacher's words

Cues and quotes, with song lyrics filtered out so you keep what your teacher said, not what the singer sang.

Your teachers, remembered

A page for each teacher: the songs they play again and again, the phrases they return to, and every class you've taken together.

Find any moment

Search a phrase, a song or a teacher and land on that moment in the class it happened.

How your body moved

Heart rate and recovery from Apple Watch alongside the class, plus a monthly look at who you practised with and the music that carried it.

Private by design

Audio is processed in memory on your iPhone and never saved or uploaded. Your class library stays on your device.
