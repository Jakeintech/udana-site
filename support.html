Udana Support

Frequently asked questions and help

What does Udana do during a live class?

Udana listens to the class through your iPhone's microphone and, entirely on-device, identifies the songs being played, transcribes what the instructor says, and detects spoken cues. Nothing is recorded to disk — the audio is analyzed in memory and discarded as the class happens.

Is my audio ever sent to a server?

No. Audio capture, song identification (via ShazamKit), speech recognition, sound analysis, and post-session summarization (via Apple's on-device FoundationModels) all run on your iPhone. Raw microphone audio never leaves your device and is never stored.

What does Udana read from Apple Health?

With your permission, Udana reads workout data, heart rate, and active energy to enrich your class recap and trend analytics. It can also write Udana sessions back to Apple Health as workouts so they appear alongside your other activity. You can revoke access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

Why does Udana ask for my location?

Coarse location is used to auto-tag the studio or venue where a class happens. Location stays on your device; it is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can deny or revoke it at any time, and Udana will keep working without venue auto-tagging.

How does nearby peer discovery work?

Udana uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework to find other Udana users physically close to you — for example, classmates in the same studio — over Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi. It's peer-to-peer, with no central server involved, and it only runs when you've turned it on.

What do I get with Udana Pro?

Udana Pro unlocks premium features across the Class Capture, Review, and Analytics experience. It's $6.99 per month or $49.99 per year, and the annual plan includes a 1-week free trial for eligible new subscribers. Subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions. Find Udana Pro in the list and choose Cancel Subscription. If you cancel a free trial before it ends, you will not be charged.

Can I export my class playlist or workout to other apps?

Yes. You can export class playlists to Apple Music or Spotify and export workouts to Strava. When you do, Udana sends only the content you've authorized (track list or workout summary) to the service you choose. You can disconnect those integrations at any time from within Udana.

How do I delete my data?

Your class history, health data, and preferences live on your device, so deleting them is under your control. You can delete individual sessions or clear your entire history from within the app, and uninstalling Udana removes the App Group container and all locally stored data. HealthKit samples written by Udana can be removed from the Health app.
