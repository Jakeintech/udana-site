Udāna Support

Frequently asked questions and help

What happens when I tap Start?

Udāna keeps the class for you. While the IN CLASS banner shows, it uses your iPhone's microphone to recognize each song and gather your teacher's words into a memory of the class. When you end the class you'll find the playlist in order, the theme your teacher wove through it, the lines worth keeping, and your heart rate if you wear Apple Watch.

Is the sound of my class saved anywhere?

No. Sound is understood on your iPhone in the moment, then let go. Song recognition (ShazamKit), speech recognition, sound analysis and the class summary (Apple's on-device models) all run on your iPhone. Class audio is never saved and never uploaded.

What does Udāna read from Apple Health?

With your permission, Udāna reads heart rate and workouts to show heart-rate zones and recovery for each class, and saves the class to Apple Health as a workout. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

Why does Udāna ask for my location?

Only to name the studio each class was at. Your location stays on your iPhone and is never used for advertising. Udāna works fine without it.

How does sharing to an Instagram Story work?

After class, open Share and choose Instagram Story. Udāna hands the class card (and a background) straight to the Instagram app, where you can move it, add to it and post it yourself. Udāna doesn't sign in to Instagram or post anything for you. Without Instagram, the same image opens in the usual share sheet.

How does nearby discovery work?

During a class, Udāna can find a teacher using Udāna in the same studio, over Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi, so tagging who taught takes one tap. Only your display name and class type are shared, device to device, with no server in between. It runs only while a class is going, and iOS asks your permission first; you can change that any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

What does Udāna Pro include?

Udāna is free to download and includes 3 classes a month. Udāna Pro removes the limit. It's $6.99 a month or $49.99 a year, and the yearly plan includes a 1-week free trial for new subscribers. Every class you've kept stays yours either way.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Udāna Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel a free trial at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged.

Can I take my playlists and workouts to other apps?

Yes. Save a class playlist to Apple Music or Spotify, or send the workout to Strava. Udāna sends only what you choose, straight from your iPhone to that service, and you can disconnect them any time in Settings.

How do I delete my classes?

Your classes live on your iPhone. Delete any class from its page or from your class list. Deleting the Udāna app removes everything it kept. Workouts Udāna saved to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.
